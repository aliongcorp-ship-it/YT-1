## Why this name (and not another "explainer" name)

The stickman explainer space is crowded, and 2025–2026 filled it with machine-made "ancient humans" videos that audiences now openly call slop. Channels are being punished for it in comments and retention. The name therefore had to do three jobs at once:

| Job | How "INKWITNESS" does it |
|---|---|
| Signal the craft | "Ink" names the visual medium. It promises drawing, not rendering. That is the exact opposite of what the low-effort competition promises. |
| Signal the genre | "Witness" signals testimony, evidence, real events — documentary, not listicle. |
| Give the character a role | Wick is the **witness**. He is in every scene, watching, recording, holding the only light. The name and the character become the same idea, so the branding is cheap to repeat and hard to copy. |

**Positioning line (internal):** *Kurzgesagt's care, The Paint Explainer's pace, a 1973 inkwell.*

## Content pillars (what episode 2, 3, 4… should be)

Every pillar is chosen because it has (a) a real, checkable record, (b) a clock, and (c) an ending you cannot guess from the title.

1. **Deep water & caves** — Pisces III (ep01), Thai cave 2018, the Byford Dolphin, Nutty Putty.
2. **Machines that failed politely** — Hyatt Regency walkway, Tenerife, the Gimli Glider, Soyuz 11.
3. **Vanishing without a trace** — Flannan Isles, the Mary Celeste, the Sodder children.
4. **Decisions under pressure** — Chernobyl's divers, Apollo 13, the 1971 Andes survivors.

**Do not** make: ancient-humans speculation, "every X explained" listicles, or anything where the hook is invented. That is the slop lane, and it is already saturated and increasingly down-ranked.

## Publishing cadence & growth plan

- **Week 1–4:** 1 episode/week, same day, same hour. The algorithm needs a rhythm before it needs volume.
- **Shorts:** cut 3 vertical 25–40s clips per episode from the strongest beats (the 12-minutes reveal, the failed hook, the hatch opening). Shorts are the cheapest subscriber acquisition for a channel with no catalogue.
- **Title testing:** YouTube's native "Test & Compare" on thumbnails from episode 3 onwards; never change the title in the first 7 days, the test audience is still being built.
- **Community:** pinned comment on every video with one verifiable source link and one question. Comments are the cheapest retention signal in 2026.
