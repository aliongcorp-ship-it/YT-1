They had 72 hours of air. They were on the bottom for 76.

29 August 1973. 150 miles south-west of Cork, the cable ship Vickers Voyager was
laying a transatlantic telephone line. When the towline fouled the aft hatch of
the submersible Pisces III, two men went to the seabed at 480 metres — and
stayed there for three days while three navies tried to reach them.

This is the deepest successful submersible rescue ever completed, told with the
record, the numbers, and the 12 minutes that were left.
