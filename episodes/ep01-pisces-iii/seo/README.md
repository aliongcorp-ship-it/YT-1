## 1. What the research says (and how it shaped this packaging)

| Finding from current platform data | What we did because of it |
|---|---|
| In 2026, the algorithm judges packaging on **CTR × retention** together; a click nobody keeps is a negative signal. Healthy browse CTR is roughly 4–6%, and the first 30 seconds must *prove* the promise. ([OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-ctr-optimization), [YTGrowth](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-ctr)) | The first 20 seconds state the place, the depth, the two names, and the 12-minute oxygen figure — exactly what the thumbnail promises. Payoff inside the first half-minute, no logo intro, no "hey guys". |
| Winning titles in 2026 are **curiosity + clarity**: a real gap, plus a clear promise. Pure curiosity without clarity kills average view duration. ([YouTubeNiche 2026 guide](https://youtubeniches.com/blog/youtube-algorithm-explained-2026-complete-guide)) | Title carries two concrete numbers (1,575 ft / 12 minutes) and one implied question (how?). Nothing is invented — both numbers are checkable. |
| The dominant format in this niche packages as *"Every X explained in N minutes"* / *"The worst X ever"*. It works, but the format is now heavily saturated and publicly labelled slop, especially the AI "ancient humans" wave. ([r/youtube](https://www.reddit.com/r/youtube/comments/1v7cl0s/has_anyone_else_noticed_all_of_these_ai_science/), [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/thepaintexplainer)) | We keep the *pace and body language* of that format (single subject, hard numbers, 6–7 min) but drop the listicle shell. Our differentiation is **verifiability + hand-inked art**, stated openly in the description. |
| Single-subject survival/disaster stories ("one disaster, told minute by minute") are pulling strong watch-to-end in mid-2026, with a documented appetite for trapped-underground/undersea rescues. ([NoodleTomato](https://www.noodletomato.com/blog/faceless-youtube-channel-ideas)) | Chose a single 76-hour clock instead of a "5 rescues" listicle. One clock = one reason to stay to the end. |
| Stickman/animated explainer channels are still breaking out with small catalogues (100K–600K views per video from 10–40 uploads). ([Taletok](https://taletok.io/blog/best-faceless-youtube-channels/)) | Format is viable now; the barrier is craft, not saturation. Hence the ink identity and the fact-checked script. |

## 2. Title

**Primary (ship this):**

```
1,575 Feet Down. 12 Minutes of Air Left.
```

Why: two specific, verifiable numbers; a gap (why would air run out?); zero hype words; reads in a feed at 120 px; 44 characters, so it never truncates on mobile.

**Alternates, in order, for Test & Compare after 7 days:**

1. `The Deepest Rescue in History Ended With 12 Minutes of Air`
2. `76 Hours at the Bottom of the Atlantic`
3. `They Opened the Hatch With 12 Minutes of Air Left`

Do **not** use: "shocking", "you won't believe", "scientists baffled", ALL CAPS words, or emoji. They raise CTR and destroy retention in this niche.
